Master's Thesis · UPM
AccessDetect — Infrastructure Detection for Accessibility
Comparative study of one-stage and two-stage object detection architectures (YOLOv10, Faster R-CNN, and others) on a custom dataset of urban accessibility features: ramps, stairs, handrails, and potholes. Identified YOLOv10L as optimal for real-time deployment based on mAP and inference speed. Built a full Streamlit application for live image-based detection.
